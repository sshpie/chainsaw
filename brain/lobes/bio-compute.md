# Bio and Compute lobe

## What this lobe is for
Load this lobe when the task is a bridge between living systems and computation, in either direction. One direction is compute-on-biology: turn sequence, structure, interaction, and expression data into predictions (graph inference, dynamic-programming alignment, GPU kernels, statistical pattern recognition). The other is biology-as-compute: borrow the algorithm from the organism (genetic algorithms, PSO, ACO, biogeography, DNA computing, immune systems, cellular automata) and run a gradient-free search. Reach for it for bioinformatics pipelines, network biology, structural modeling, any metaheuristic or evolutionary optimizer, and any time a problem is stochastic search over noisy data on parallel hardware.

## The through-line
Both halves are the same machine seen from two ends: stochastic search and parallel compute over noisy, high-dimensional biological data. Books 55, 56, 57, 60, 61, 63 point compute at biology; 58, 62, 64, 65 point biology back at compute; 59 is the silicon both run on. The unifying discipline is verification-first: an interactome is 45-90% false-positive before you clean it (#57), a classifier accuracy is a lie without honest imbalanced-data evaluation (#56, #61), and a metaheuristic's result is noise until its convergence is proven, not just observed (#64). This is the  verify-stage thesis in a different domain: the scan (or the raw Y2H screen, or the un-tuned optimizer) produces candidates; the load-bearing work is refuting them.

## Operating moves
- Cell-list spatial hashing for O(1) neighbor search: floor((coord - min)/maxR) to an (i,j,k) key, dict maps key to atom list, query only the 27 surrounding buckets. Build once, reuse verbatim for any 3D proximity problem, not just molecules. (#63)
- Think in arithmetic intensity (OP/B), not FLOPs: a kernel is bound by DRAM bandwidth until tiling into __shared__ with __syncthreads() strip-mining raises reuse. 16x16 tile = 4 OP/B; halo/ghost cells lean on L2. In production call cuBLAS/CUTLASS, do not hand-roll GEMM. (#59)
- Pick a search by structure, not metaphor: 20 optimizers collapse onto one 9-step skeleton (init, evaluate, select, perturb, elitism, iterate). PSO v = omega*v + C1*rand*(Pbest-X) + C2*rand*(Gbest-X), C1=C2=2. SA accepts worse with exp(-delta/lambda). Code the skeleton, swap the operator. (#62)
- Clean an interactome before you trust it: estimate FP/FN with the d'Haeseleer-Church model, filter by co-expression (r about 0.3), intersect high-confidence sets, use a biophysics null to flag edges that are only hydrophobic stickiness. Verification precedes every downstream claim. (#57)
- Prove a metaheuristic converges: build the Markov transition matrix from the multinomial theorem, confirm primitivity, bound time-to-optimum from the spectral radius rho of the non-elite block; lower mutation lowers rho and speeds convergence. Observed convergence is not proven convergence. (#64)
- Rank influence at O(n^2), not 2^n: column-stochastic influence matrix from Boolean partial derivatives f(xj=0) XOR f(xj=1), power-iterate with PageRank teleport damping. The exponential blow-up is usually avoidable. (#60)
- Design data integration by the warehouse-vs-federation-vs-mediator axis: wrappers over autonomous sources, cost-based push-down (DiscoveryLink CREATE WRAPPER/SERVER/NICKNAME, tune PUSHDOWN/MAX_TABS). Sources silently change format about every six months; build for drift. (#55)
- GA feature selection as a default wrapper: chromosome = binary feature mask, fitness = classifier accuracy, tournament/rank select, crossover about 0.5, mutation about 0.05. Same skeleton trains ANN weights (flatten every layer into one chromosome). (#65, #61)
- Compute with molecules when the problem is combinatorial: Adleman encodes a Hamiltonian path in 20-base oligos and reads it off a gel; Lipton solves SAT in linear operations over 2^n parallel strands; Shapiro's FokI automaton computes in enzyme. Massive parallelism, not speed per operation. (#58)
- Encode protein signal before you classify it: one-hot, BLOSUM62 rows, PSI-BLAST PSSM, DSSP 8-to-3 secondary structure, solvent ASA, disorder; then SVM with an RBF kernel and a grid search over C and gamma. The encoding sets the accuracy ceiling, not the classifier. (#56)

## How the books interlock
Read the substrate first when the task is heavy: 59 (GPU) is the engine under 56's cryoEM reconstruction and 63's O(n^2) energy double-sums. Then the domain: 57 is the graph-theory foundation 56, 60, and 61 all stand on; 55 is the data layer that feeds every one of them. The optimization cluster is self-reinforcing: 62 is the breadth survey of the whole metaheuristic family, 64 is the single-algorithm deep dive (BBO) with the convergence proofs the survey omits, and 65 is the applied layer that wires GA/PSO/ACO onto real medical data. 58 sits above all of them as first principles: it is where the GA, the neural net, the immune system, and the cellular automaton are each reduced to a named algorithm and an honest failure mode before any application chapter uses them.

## Collective lift from other lobes
- #03 The Art of Randomness [ai-agentic] is the single most-linked cross-lobe synapse in this lobe: ten of eleven bio books lean on RNG quality they never examine. Every roulette-wheel spin, PSO velocity draw, GA mutation, BBO immigration test, MCMC/ABC sampler, and GPU parallel-RNG stream rides on it. Pull #03 to prime any stochastic-optimizer work.
- #12 Open Source Fuzzing Tools / #42 From Day Zero to Zero Day [offensive]: a coverage-guided fuzzer IS a population-based stochastic search over program inputs. 58 supplies the schema theorem and building-block hypothesis, 62 and 64 supply the convergence theory, that the fuzzing books use operationally but never derive. The bridge runs both ways.
- #32 Hacking Cryptography [offensive]: rests on NP-hardness and Shannon entropy; 58's DNA computing attacks exactly those NP problems (Adleman/Lipton), and 57/60's mutual-information edge inference (REVEAL, ARACNE, Infogenomics) is the same H = -sum(p log p) machinery.
- #24 Securing AI Systems [defensive]: the model attack surface for the SVM/RF/BRNN/ANN classifiers these books train and deploy on PHI (55, 56, 65 all name HIPAA/GDPR as afterthoughts). 59 is even the literal compute substrate (im2col, cuDNN) under #24's ML-security concerns.
- #20 Practical Fairness [ai-agentic]: fair model training is constrained optimization (accuracy subject to demographic parity); 62 and 64 supply the penalty-function and Pareto-front machinery, and 56/60 meet it on imbalanced-data evaluation and binning-choice sensitivity.
- #08 Craft foundations [craft-code]: 55's declarative wrapper languages (CPL, Daplex, Icarus) and 63's StructBio toolkit (Shell, Overlapper, GraphBase) are masterclasses in reusable single-responsibility tooling; #21 [ai-agentic] shares the exact edit-distance DP of Needleman-Wunsch and the text-mining of gene prioritization; #44 [offensive] shares the subgraph-isomorphism and shortest-path graph algorithms.

## Reach for the lobe when
- The task is any bioinformatics, computational-biology, systems-biology, or network-biology pipeline
- You need a gradient-free optimizer (GA, PSO, ACO, DE, SA, BBO, firefly, grey wolf) and want to pick it by structure, not by its metaphor
- The work is GPU or massively-parallel: mapping a problem onto SMs, warps, and the memory hierarchy
- A problem is stochastic search over a noisy fitness surface, in any domain, and you want the convergence and RNG rigor under it
- You are inferring or cleaning a graph (PPI, metabolic, regulatory) and need the false-positive discipline first
- You are modeling molecular structure, running alignment, or scripting a viewer into real computation
- A biology-inspired primitive (DNA computing, immune system, cellular automaton, artificial life) is the right tool and you need it reduced to its algorithm and failure mode
