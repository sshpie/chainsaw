# Human and Influence lobe

## What this lobe is for
Load it when the target, asset, or gatekeeper is a human, not a host: pretexting a help desk, moving a stonewalling triager, leading a long engagement team, or modeling the social-engineering vector against a client. Also load it for the inverse defensive job - building awareness programs and red-flag triage - and as the human template for orchestrating subagents.

## The through-line
One toolkit, two polarities. The exact mechanics that build legitimate trust - listening below the words, matching then leading, vulnerability before trust, credibility before logic, rapport as the channel and content as the cover - are the same mechanics a social engineer weaponizes. Every book here is either the white-hat or the black-hat reading of one substrate: humans decide emotionally and justify rationally, and the data that matters lives below the role/status layer. Master the helper and the attacker as one skill.

## Operating moves
- Walk the Ladder of Inference back to observable data to refute your own framing before it hardens into a finding [5]. The cognitive twin of verification-is-load-bearing.
- Pick and name a stance before speaking - Heron's six, Facilitative ~75-80% [5]; default Facilitative, go Directive only when the ZPD requires it.
- Sequence influence strictly: Credibility (Trust + domain-relevant Expertise) to Emotion to Logic to Action [14]. A correct tactic in the wrong layer fails; leading with logic is the top failure.
- Identify your lab coats: lead disclosure with CVE/CERT track record, not the payload [14]. Offer 3-5 fix paths with a default, never 1 (single-option aversion) or 12 (choice overload) [14].
- Never drop "because": If/When-Then-Because moved compliance 60% to 93% [14]. Time the send (Kairos): parole odds ~0% pre-lunch, ~65% after [14].
- Run elicitation up the talk-type ladder Thing-Action-Head-Heart-Soul [45]; manage your own state first - tense body, blocked mind [45].
- Map the human attack onto the 6-stage life cycle; pretext = contextual relevance + emotional appeal + credibility + target-knowledge [19]. Verify via a separate approved channel; against a live impersonator feed false answers, do not confront [19].
- Run the Sentiments gut-check to label a relationship L-1/L1/L2; apathy means you are at L1 and complex work leaks information there [9]. Use "I intend to" language for assertive specs [9].
- Feedback at 5:1 / BET-for-positive, BEAR-for-critical [5][22]; specific and meaning-tied, "slide 9 made the gap clearer," not "great job" [22].
- Run the Blue Tape List on any new target/codebase; speak last to gather context [11].

## How the books interlock
Start with [9] for relationship-level theory (why data hides below status), [11] for the daily small-acts mechanics that move a relationship up a level, [10]/[22] for team composition and measurable humility. Drop into [5] for structured one-on-one craft, [45] for the lighter conversational substrate beneath it. For any influence-without-authority push, run [14] as the state machine and [45] as its delivery layer. [19] is the dark mirror over all of them - read it against [14]+[45] to model both sides of a pretext end to end.

## Collective lift from other lobes
- [13] Human Factors and [16] Psybersecurity (defensive) formalize the threat model - stressed operators, anxiety-blocks-learning, the cognitive conditions that make a human exploitable; this lobe gives the intervention, they give the conditions to target or harden.
- [42] From Day Zero to Zero Day and [43] Kali (offensive) carry the chain past the human vector: pretext from [14]/[19] opens initial access, those drive it to technical impact.
- [38] Agentic Coding and [39] Architectural Patterns (ai-agentic) take the payload directly - L1-vs-L2 task specs, Pygmalion framing, and gradual release of responsibility are coaching for machines; "intelligence at input" is the descending O-to-S-to-H orchestration result on the people side.

## Reach for the lobe when
- A finding has to move someone who owes you nothing (triager, CERT, vendor).
- You are pretexting, eliciting, or modeling the human attack surface of an engagement.
- A team, handoff, or agent pipeline is starving on data that lives below the role layer.
- Running an awareness program, red-flag triage, or deepfake/spoofing defense.
- Delivering hard feedback or a high-friction disclosure conversation.
