# Philosophy lobe

## What this lobe is for
Load this lobe when a task needs judgment, not a scan: deciding which finding actually matters, how grave it is, how to frame it without fearmongering, and whether the system's security problem is structural rather than a missing control. Reach for it before disclosure framing, severity calls, "is this AI-for-good claim real," and any moment where the operator (not the tool) is the load-bearing variable.

## The through-line
Ethics is structural, not a bolt-on. Book 1 interrogates why a system was built the way it was (by whom, why, for what), Book 2 interrogates how to reason about its security and stakes. Together: the converted, thoughtful operator is the load-bearing intervention, because innovation always precedes the law and security is never absolute. You raise an adversary's cost and you change judgments; you do not patch your way to safety.

## Operating moves
- Run the cost-imposition test on every finding: state how much it raises or lowers adversary cost, frame remediation as cost-raising not "fix the vuln." (#2)
- Classify into the CIA triad before assigning severity, so impact language is anchored not adjectival; then run the proportionality filter (is "sophisticated" load-bearing or decorative). (#2)
- Use graded attribution: state confidence, account for false-flag, decide whether public naming even serves a purpose before naming. (#2)
- Walk the lateral-movement question explicitly (tram example): does a peripheral system reach the safety-critical one. That pivot is the finding that matters. (#2)
- Run eidetic variation as a threat-surface and externality enumerator: vary the artifact's essence, materials, uses, abandonment, recycling; ask what it replaced and "why should this exist on the market." (#1)
- Apply the epoche: suspend the natural attitude (the default "is it profitable" or an agent's reward objective) to reach a new region of being where new options appear. The philosophical twin of "null result means generate variants." (#1)
- Name the bivium fork explicitly (smooth profit road vs steep caring road) before picking a side; challenge the framing first. (#1)
- Use the three-stage degeneration schema (nameless -> profitable -> crush-performance) to diagnose tools and vendors hollowed out by incentive capture. (#1)

## How the books interlock
Read #1 first to fix the ontological stance, then #2 to ground it in security stakes and proportion. #1 supplies "question the actor not the process"; #2 supplies the cost-imposition, kill-chain, CIA, and graded-attribution machinery that turns the stance into a defensible severity call. They are the judgment substrate the other 43 cards plug into: #1 asks why it was built this way, #2 asks how grave it is and how to say so.

## Reach for the lobe when
- A finding needs a severity call and you feel the reflex to write "critical" or "sophisticated"
- You are about to attribute an operator publicly
- An "AI for good" / "responsible AI" / "ethical-by-design" claim needs stress-testing
- The problem looks structural (incentive capture, frictionless adoption) not control-shaped
- You are deciding whether a peripheral-to-critical pivot is the real finding
- The deliverable is convincing a CISO or coaching a team, not dumping a CVE list
