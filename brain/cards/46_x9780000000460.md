## Principles

- The banner is the basic unit: Shodan indexes service banners, not hosts, so a single IP exposing many services returns many results - the index is service-population-shaped, exactly the lens for surveying a platform across the Internet
- Filters narrow, facets aggregate: filters (product:, port:, net:, vuln:, ssl.version:) carve the subpopulation; facets (ssh.fingerprint, http.component, country) give the distribution that turns a count into a finding before any host is touched
- Search is banner-text-only and recent-only (last 30 days) by default; metadata lives behind credit-spending filters and the data is a moving snapshot - reproducibility means download-then-parse, not re-query
- Cascading and history betray structure: a banner can leak peers (DHT, Bitcoin, Elastic nodes) and the crawler chases them via _shodan.referrer, while history=True surfaces a host's indexed past - one seed expands into a provenance graph and a deception cannot hide its origin
- Honeypots and obscurity are both measurable: duplicate serials, too many services, implausible cloud hosting and a bad banner history betray fakes, and non-standard ports cluster predictably - absence of a default is not absence of exposure
