# 43. The Ultimate Kali Linux Book, 3rd Edition

**ISBN** 9781835085806  |  **Lobe** offensive

> The full-engagement spine: a build-the-lab-yourself walkthrough that chains passive recon to Active Directory domain dominance, anchored to Cyber Kill Chain / MITRE ATT&CK / PTES and a 7-phase model, so it is the index card for "which Kali tool and which framework stage covers this move."

## Reach for this when

- You need the canonical tool+command for a standard offensive step (MSFvenom encoding, Impacket psexec, Responder, ntlmrelayx, secretsdump, aircrack-ng) and want it framework-anchored rather than copy-pasted from a blog.
- You are walking an AD compromise and need the continuous escalation chain laid out: hashdump -> Pass-the-Hash -> NTLM relay -> Kerberoasting -> golden/silver tickets -> skeleton key, with the exact Hashcat modes (-m 1000 NTLM, -m 5600 NTLMv2, -m 13100 Kerberoasting TGS).
- You are standing up an isolated practice lab and want a reproducible multi-network design (PentestNet 172.30.1.0/24, HiddenNet 10.11.12.0/24, RedTeamLab 192.168.42.0/24) that forces pivoting through a dual-homed host.
- You need to map a finding onto a recognized framework stage for a report (Cyber Kill Chain phase, MITRE ATT&CK technique, OWASP Top 10 2021 category, CVSS severity).
- You want a fast OWASP Top 10 2021 checklist with each category bound to a concrete lab challenge (Juice Shop SQLi auth bypass, WebGoat SSRF/A09 logging, DVWA sqlmap dump).
- You need the engagement-discipline scaffolding: written permission as the get-out-of-jail card, SOW/NDA/RoE, the stop-and-report-to-law-enforcement rule, and report writing for non-technical executives.

## Principles

- The pentest is a disciplined lifecycle, not ad-hoc hacking: every action maps onto the Cyber Kill Chain, MITRE ATT&CK, PTES, and a 7-phase engagement (pre-engagement, recon, enumeration, vuln assessment, exploitation, post-exploitation, reporting). The framework is the load-bearing part, not the tool.
- Credentials, hashes, and tickets are the universal currency of post-exploitation: one continuous escalation story, not a bag of single CVEs. The foothold is cheap; the chain to domain dominance is the work.
- The chain is worth more than the sum of findings: the lab is deliberately built so HiddenNet is reachable ONLY by compromising the dual-homed Metasploitable3 host, making pivoting/autoroute a first-class skill rather than a footnote.
- Legal authorization is the prerequisite, not the disclaimer: scope, RoE, and reporting are as much the craft as the exploits; covering tracks and logging/SIEM (the A09 lesson) are taught from both sides.
- Tooling is framework-anchored, not tool-worship: Empire = listeners/stagers/agents, Burp = intercepting-proxy + Repeater/Intruder, BloodHound = Neo4j attack graph. Learn the concept beneath the tool so you can swap tools.
- Each defensive generation closes the prior attack and opens a new one (WEP->WPA->WPA2->WPA3; PMF/SAE defeat deauth-driven handshake capture, forcing downgrade). Treat the arms race as the model, not any one break.

## Moves

- AD credential chain end to end: dump SAM/NTDS.dit (secretsdump), crack with John/Hashcat (-m 1000), Pass-the-Hash with Impacket psexec/PTH-WinExe/xFreeRDP, poison LLMNR/NBT-NS with Responder for NTLMv2 (-m 5600), relay to SMB with ntlmrelayx when SMB signing is off, abuse IPv6 with mitm6 to relay to LDAPS.
- Kerberos abuse: enumerate SPNs and Kerberoast with GetUserSPNs.py (-m 13100), forge golden tickets from the krbtgt hash (kerberos::golden, survives a krbtgt password change), silver tickets, and skeleton key (misc::skeleton, password 'mimikatz', lost on DC reboot because it lives in LSASS).
- Stand up C2 with PowerShell Empire 5 (listener -> stager -> agent), run Mimikatz/bypassuac/psinject, apply a malleable profile (windows-updates) for traffic blending, drive via Starkiller GUI; alternative Meterpreter post-ex (getsystem, incognito token impersonation, migrate, autoroute pivot, clearev).
- Map and traverse AD with PowerView (Get-NetDomain, Get-DomainSID, Find-LocalAdminAccess, Invoke-ShareFinder) and visualize attack paths with BloodHound via SharpHound / BloodHound-Python over Neo4j.
- Payload evasion: MSFvenom shikata_ga_nai (-i 20), Shellter PE injection into a legitimate binary against signature AV; understand signature vs behavioral vs heuristic detection and that modern AV/EDR often still catches it.
- Web chain: Burp via FoxyProxy at 127.0.0.1:8080, ' OR '1'='1 / fake@email.local' OR 1=1; -- auth bypass in Juice Shop, Intruder + rockyou.txt for brute, sqlmap --dbs/--tables/--columns/--dump on DVWA, BeEF browser hooking for client-side payloads.
- Wireless: airodump-ng recon, aireplay-ng deauth to capture the WPA2 4-way handshake, aircrack-ng + wordlist; against WPA3 use a downgrade plus dictionary attack because PMF defeats deauth; evil twin / enterprise via airgeddon + FreeRADIUS.
- Build the reproducible lab: VBoxManage dhcpserver add for the three isolated networks, Vagrant-built Metasploitable3 (rapid7/metasploitable3-win2k8 dual-homed + ub1404 Linux), and ZeroTier push-through VPN + XRDP for remote engagement access.

## Watch out

- Breadth over depth by design: it teaches the Kali tool surface broadly and shallowly. The synthesis itself says treat it as the spine, not the skeleton. Do not mistake the survey command for mastery of the technique.
- Lab-target realism gap: exploits are demonstrated on intentionally vulnerable boxes (Metasploitable 2/3, Juice Shop, WebGoat, DVWA) on isolated networks. EternalBlue/vsFTPd-style one-shots and unsegmented AD are training scaffolds; real targets are patched, segmented, and monitored.
- Weakest exactly where modern engagements live: the synthesis names modern EDR evasion, cloud/container pentesting, and mobile as its thin spots. The shikata_ga_nai/Shellter evasion is dated; the book itself notes modern AV often still catches it. Do not lean on it for EDR bypass.
- Edition currency: tool versions, default ports, and CVE relevance drift fast. Verify the current Impacket/Responder/Empire/Hashcat syntax and mode numbers against the live tool before relying on a command from the text.
- Framework-anchored phrasing can read as box-ticking: mapping a finding to a Cyber Kill Chain phase or OWASP category is reporting scaffolding, not evidence. The label is not the verified 200-with-data read; do not let the framework slot substitute for proof.
