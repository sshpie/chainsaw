# 47. OSINT Techniques: Resources for Uncovering Online Information (11th Ed.)

**ISBN** x9780000000477  |  **Lobe** offensive

> Bazzell and Edison's canonical OSINT bible: a clean-room investigative environment, a full identifier-pivot arsenal (domain, IP, email, username, breach, leak, stealer log), and a triage-to-report workflow with an explicit restraint ethic, the reference we reach for when an IP or domain has to become an attributed human operator.

## Reach for this when

- We have a Shodan or Censys hit and need to walk the IP back to a human operator: ViewDNS reverse-IP and IP-history, Whoxy and Whoisology historical Whois to defeat privacy masking, Bing ip: co-host enumeration, the exact pivot chain that turns a bare host into a named registrant
- We pulled a TLS cert CN or favicon hash off an AI-infra host and need the next pivot: reverse-Whois by registrant email and phone, co-hosted-domain clustering, IP-history to find the operator's older un-masked records
- We are building or hardening a custom NuClide recon tool and want the doctrine: self-host your own search tools, never trust a third-party aggregator that can vanish or log you, derive the static query URL behind any site's form and wire it into automation (the garlic toolkit and aimap dork tables are this chapter's principle made code)
- We are setting up the operating posture for an assessment: disposable per-investigation VM, VPN exit in the target's region, covert-account hygiene, contamination isolation so evidence stays clean for disclosure
- We hit exposed breach data, a data leak, or stealer-log content during recon and need the investigative-benefit framing plus the handling discipline (what the artifact reveals about a target's stack and OPSEC, and where the legal/ethical line sits)
- We need to articulate scope, intent, and restraint on a finding: the misconfigured-Elasticsearch-no-credentials scenario and intent test is the verbatim NuClide enumerate-not-exfiltrate ethic, useful for framing a disclosure and a report's authorization section

## Principles

- Self-reliance over third-party tools: any external search site can disappear, get a takedown, or log your queries, so own your environment (disposable Debian VM, per-case isolation) and own your tooling (self-hosted HTML query tools you can edit, extend, and trust)
- Information is not intelligence until it has context: triage and articulate the question first, verify the identifiers you were handed, then drive a repeatable Discover-pivot-verify-document workflow rather than bush-whacking the internet
- Every identifier pivots to every other identifier: domain to registrant to email to username to phone to breach record to physical address, and historical records (Whois history, Wayback, IP history) defeat the privacy masking that hides the current state
- Preserve everything the moment you find it: online data is ephemeral, so capture with provenance (screenshot, PDF, metadata, timestamp) into an organized case notebook because the artifact, not the path, is the finding
- Restraint is the ethic, not the constraint: intent and articulable justification decide whether legally-accessible-but-mistakenly-exposed data (an unauthenticated Elasticsearch) is appropriate to use, and reasonable expectation of privacy is the test you run on yourself
