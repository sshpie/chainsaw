# 56. Algorithmic and Artificial Intelligence Methods for Protein Bioinformatics

**ISBN** 9781118567814  |  **Lobe** bio-compute

> A chapter-by-chapter field manual of the concrete algorithms that turn protein sequence, structure, and interaction data into predictions: SVM/HMM/NN/RF/fuzzy classifiers over BLOSUM/PSSM/DSSP encodings, GPU-parallel cryoEM reconstruction, structure and network alignment by subgraph isomorphism, spectral, and Lagrangian relaxation, ACO/GA module detection, and fractal sequence representations, each pinned to real datasets and accuracy numbers.

## Reach for this when

- Encoding a protein sequence window for a classifier (orthogonal 20-bit, BLOSUM62, PSI-BLAST PSSM, HSSP profile, physicochemical, VSL2 disorder) and choosing SVM vs HMM vs BRNN vs Random Forest over it.
- Predicting a per-residue structural label (secondary structure Q3 H/E/C, solvent accessibility, disulfide bonding, cysteine oxidation state) and wanting the staged NN -> SVM -> CRF progression and its metrics.
- Fighting severe class imbalance in a bio classifier (few positives) with GSVM-RU repetitive undersampling, granular or fuzzy SVM, and cost-sensitive learning instead of raw accuracy tuning.
- Aligning two protein structures or contact maps (CMO, LCP) when Needleman-Wunsch DP is not enough and you need spectral (Al-Eigen, EIGAS) or Lagrangian (A_purva) relaxation.
- Comparing or aligning biological networks (PPI, metabolic): functional module detection, subgraph isomorphism, IsoRank/GRAAL/NetworkBLAST, motif finding by z-score and p-value.
- Porting a heavy reconstruction or scoring kernel to GPU (cryoEM 3D, sparse SpMV) with CUDA plus OpenMP multilevel parallelism and an ELLPACK-R sparse layout.
- Indexing a large structure or sequence corpus for similarity search with suffix trees, torsion-angle strings, and a TF-IDF vector space with cosine similarity.
- Feature-selecting and honestly evaluating a QSAR or biomarker model (t-test, PCA, mRMR; MCC, AUC-ROC, sensitivity/specificity, LOOCV and k-fold, GDT_TS).

## Principles

- Encoding beats classifier choice. Evolutionary profiles (PSI-BLAST PSSM, HSSP) lift accuracy more than swapping SVM for NN; cysteine oxidation prediction climbed from 71% to 94.2% mainly by adding PSSM plus dipeptide composition plus structural context, not by a new learner.
- The sliding window is the default abstraction: a residue is classified from a symmetric window of its encoded neighbors, and window size is a tuned hyperparameter alongside kernel C and gamma.
- Imbalance is the silent accuracy killer; report MCC and g-means, not raw accuracy. GSVM-RU repetitively undersamples the majority, granular and fuzzy SVM partition the majority into informative granules.
- Core comparison problems are NP-hard: Contact Map Overlap (CMO, Goldman) and subgraph isomorphism for network and motif matching. The book answers with principled relaxation (Lagrangian in A_purva, spectral eigen relaxation, SDP for graph cuts) or FPT and color coding, never brute force.
- Structure and network problems reduce to eigenvectors and dynamic programming. The Fiedler vector (second Laplacian eigenvector) bisects PPI graphs; Umeyama and Al-Eigen spectral assignment aligns contact matrices; double DP and Needleman-Wunsch recur throughout.
- Randomized and stochastic construction is first-class: ACO with MaxMin Ant System (pheromone evaporation/deposit) builds modules, chaos game representation and IFS map sequences to fractal points, and edge/node reshuffling produces null p-values for pathway and motif significance.
- Evaluate on nonredundant sets (PISCES at <=25% homology), with k-fold and LOOCV and structure-quality metrics (GDT_TS, Q3, MCC, AUC-ROC). A model tested on homologous data reports inflated numbers.

## Moves

- Feature encodings: orthogonal 20-bit one-hot, BLOSUM62 substitution rows, PSI-BLAST PSSM, DSSP 8->3 secondary-structure reduction (H/E/C), solvent ASA, VSL2 intrinsic disorder, dipeptide composition, biprofile Bayes, cysteine state sequences (CSS).
- Classifiers: SVM (RBF/Gaussian/polynomial/linear kernels via SVMlight and libsvm; grid search over C and gamma), HMM/Viterbi (HMMSTR), feedforward and bidirectional recurrent NN (BRNN), Random Forest, naive Bayes, KNN, GRHCRF, and IFID3/EIFID3 fuzzy decision trees.
- Cysteine oxidation pipeline (Ch11): NN -> 2/3-stage SVM -> HMM+NN hybrid -> BRNN -> GRHCRF, using PSSM plus dipeptide composition and an even-parity finite-state-automaton constraint; DISULFIND, DSSP, PISCES; accuracy 71% to 94.2%.
- Imbalance handling: GSVM-RU repetitive undersampling, granular SVM (GSVM/CSVM), K-means and fuzzy C-means granulation, cost-sensitive matrices, g-means scoring.
- cryoEM on GPU (Ch12): WBP/ART/SIRT/SART/ASART iterative reconstruction, blob (Kaiser-Bessel) basis, OpenMP-coarse plus CUDA-fine multilevel parallelism, asynchronous CUDA streams for latency hiding, Blob-ELLR (an ELLPACK-R sparse format) with 3 symmetry optimizations, on an NVIDIA Tesla C2050 Fermi with 448 SPs.
- Structure and contact-map alignment (Ch13): CMO and LCP formulations, spectral Al-Eigen and EIGAS (smooth contact matrix plus eigenvalue assignment), GOSSIP, A_purva (Lagrangian relaxation plus double DP), Needleman-Wunsch with gap-open and gap-extension penalties.
- Indexing and IR (Ch14): generalized suffix trees (Ukkonen/McCreight/Weiner linear-time), torsion-angle (phi/psi/omega) encoding, vector space model with TF-IDF and cosine similarity, PROSIMA; over PDB/SCOP/CATH/DALI.
- Network algorithms: spectral bisection via Fiedler vector and graph Laplacian (MinMaxCut, RatioCut, NormalizedCut, SDP relaxation, multilevel Kernighan-Lin and Fiduccia-Mattheyses coarsening, Ch18); ACO-FMD module detection against MCODE/RNSC/edge-betweenness with Czekanovski-Dice and PathRatio (Ch20); alignment via PathBlast/NetworkBLAST/IsoRank(N)/GRAAL/MI-GRAAL/Graemlin/MaWISh (Ch22); metabolic alignment by tree decomposition and color coding in MetNetAligner (Ch21).
- Fractal sequence representation (Ch16): HP model, chaos game representation (20/12/4-CGR, AAD-CGR), Z-curve, iterated function systems, detrended fluctuation analysis, multifractal Dq spectrum, Daubechies db2 wavelet, recurrence quantification (%REC/%DET/linemax/ENT), Fisher linear discriminant at 94.12%.
- QSAR and biomarkers (Ch23/26): granular kernel trees (GKT1/GKT2) GA-tuned (crossover 0.7, mutation 0.5, roulette-wheel selection) with RBF-SVM at 87% on DHFR pyrimidines; graph-centrality gene signatures, MammaPrint 70-gene, GeneSigDB 3515 signatures, Chuang subnetwork biomarkers, mRMR/t-test/PCA feature selection.

## Watch out

- This is an edited volume of 26 author-team chapters, not one unified pipeline; notation, encodings, and evaluation protocols shift chapter to chapter, so port ideas, not a single end-to-end recipe.
- Headline accuracies (94.2% cysteine oxidation, 94.12% fractal LDA, 87% QSAR, 91.6% MeTaDoR) are dataset-specific on small nonredundant sets; treat them as ceilings under favorable homology filtering, not field-wide numbers.
- Circa-2013 tooling: Tesla C2050/Fermi, SVMlight, VSL2, and the PSI-BLAST era. The GPU specifics and several web servers are dated; algorithms and encodings transfer, hardware numbers do not, and deep-learning structure prediction (AlphaFold-style) postdates the book.
- Most methods target NP-hard problems by relaxation or heuristics, so results are approximate and sensitive to penalty settings (gap-open/extension, pheromone evaporation, kernel C and gamma) that need per-dataset tuning.
- Class-imbalance and homology-redundancy pitfalls are easy to reintroduce; without PISCES-style filtering and MCC or g-means reporting, the numbers inflate silently.
