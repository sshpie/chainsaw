# 57. Introduction to Biological Networks

**ISBN** 9781420010367  |  **Lobe** bio-compute

> Graph-theoretic playbook for molecular biology: infer, validate, model, and mine protein-protein, metabolic, and gene-regulatory networks, from cleaning 45-90% false-positive Y2H data to power-law robust-yet-fragile topology to random-walk disease-gene prediction on the human interactome.

## Reach for this when

- Turning a noisy interaction dataset (yeast two-hybrid, co-IP/TAP, ChIP-seq) into a defensible network, and needing to estimate its false-positive rate before trusting any topology claim
- Deciding which node to hit to fragment a scale-free network fastest, or which to protect: hub vs date-hub targeting, marginal robustness, blast radius
- Inferring edges you cannot measure directly (mutual-information co-expression, phylogenetic profiles, gene fusion, interologs) and fusing them by likelihood ratio
- Predicting metabolic knockouts and gene-pair epistasis from a stoichiometric model with no kinetic parameters (flux balance analysis, MOMA)
- Finding functional modules or complexes in a large graph purely from topology (Markov clustering, Newman-Girvan modularity, spectral partitioning)
- Ranking candidate disease genes by network position (random-walk-with-restart, Laplacian diffusion kernels) and reasoning about comorbidity and synthetic-lethal drug targets
- Choosing a null model to decide whether an observed motif or module is significant (configuration model vs biophysics-based MpK/stickiness)

## Principles

- Power-law degree N(k) proportional to k^-gamma (yeast PPI exponent about 2.25) makes networks robust to random node removal but fragile to targeted hub attack; this robust-yet-fragile asymmetry is the core security property, and about 20% of the roughly 6000 yeast genes are essential with hubs enriched among them.
- Verification is load-bearing: high-throughput interactomes carry 45-90% false-positive rates (d'Haeseleer and Church); two yeast Y2H screens (Uetz, Ito) overlap on only about 150 of thousands of interactions, so single-experiment topology is untrustworthy without integration.
- Information theory grounds edge inference: Shannon entropy H = -sum(p log p) and mutual information M(X,Y) = H(X) - H(X|Y) drive REVEAL (M(G,S)/H(G) = 1) and ARACNE, which prunes indirect edges with the Data Processing Inequality.
- Metabolism is linear algebra at steady state: dX/dt = S*v + b, with S*v = 0 defining the flux null space; growth is a linear program, and MOMA (a quadratic program minimizing distance to wild-type flux) predicts knockout flux better than pure LP.
- Modularity implies robustness: Newman-Girvan Q = sum_l (e_ll - a_l^2) partitions topology into functional modules; the Drosophila segment-polarity network holds its parasegment pattern across tenfold parameter variation (0.5% of random parameter sets work, about 90% per single parameter) because of bistability from positive feedback, not fine tuning.
- Networks evolve by gene duplication, not preferential attachment: the DMC model (Vazquez 2003, dimerization p about 0.1, edge-loss q about 0.7) beat Barabasi-Albert and six other models on the Drosophila PPI (Middendorf 2005); duplication retains evolutionary memory and lets ancient networks be reconstructed (Navlakha-Kingsford).
- Disease is a network property: the diseasome (Goh 2007, 1777 disease genes, 1284 diseases, 22 disorder classes) shows shared-gene diseases have 2-4x average comorbidity; heritable disease genes sit at the periphery (essential-gene mutations are lethal) while somatic cancer genes are hubs.
- Boolean dynamics have a critical window: power-law networks with exponent 2.0 < gamma < 2.5 sit in the robust regime (Aldana-Cluzel), and the exponents of most real networks cluster exactly there.

## Moves

- Clean an interactome before trusting it: estimate FP/FN rates with the d'Haeseleer and Church error model, filter by co-expression (correlation cutoff about 0.3, Fraser-Plotkin) or high-confidence intersection (Han 2004, Reguly 2006); use the MpK biophysics null (fix M = 100, tune cutoff affinity A_C in 10^25 to 10^40 to hit exponent about 2) to flag edges that are only hydrophobic stickiness.
- Infer regulatory edges from expression: REVEAL (mutual-information Boolean rule search), ARACNE (MI plus Data Processing Inequality), CLR, or Dynamic Bayesian Networks scored by BIC S_BIC = S - (K/2) log N with EM for hidden states.
- Predict physical PPIs without an assay: phylogenetic profiles / co-evolution, Rosetta-stone gene fusion, conserved gene neighborhood/operons, correlated-mutations in-silico two-hybrid, and interologs (Walhout/Matthews, about 80% geometric-mean sequence identity); fuse by naive-Bayes likelihood ratio (Jansen 2003, yeast prior odds about 1/600, accept L > 600), pull from STRING.
- Run flux balance analysis: build stoichiometry matrix S, solve max biomass subject to S*v = 0 by linear programming (COBRA toolbox); score gene pairs by fitness W = V_growth^dX / V_growth^WT and influence epsilon = W_XY - W_X*W_Y (epsilon>0 buffering, epsilon<0 aggravating), use MOMA (QP) for the mutant flux, check monochromaticity of interactions.
- Find complexes/modules: Markov Clustering (MCL, van Dongen: alternate expansion = matrix power and inflation = elementwise power, renormalize to convergence); Newman-Girvan via the modularity matrix B_ij = A_ij - k_i k_j/(2m), recursively split on the sign of its principal eigenvector (spectral partitioning) until Q stops rising.
- Separate hub types: overlay expression to split party hubs (high neighbor co-expression) from date hubs (low); date-hub/superhub removal fragments the graph into functional modules (Han 2004) and is the high-blast-radius attack, party-hub removal is not.
- Model growth and reconstruct ancestors: simulate DMC (duplicate node, dimerize with p, delete one duplicate edge with q) or the 7-parameter GDD model (Evlampiev-Isambert); reconstruct ancient networks backward by argmax over G_{t-1} of P(G_t | G_{t-1}, M) one timestep at a time (Bayes, uniform prior).
- Rank disease genes: guilt-by-association linkage (Oti 2006, median 88 genes/locus), phenotype-closeness correlation with C_gp = sum e^(-L_gg') (Wu 2008), random-walk-with-restart on the column-normalized adjacency with restart probability r (Kohler 2008), or ab-initio Laplacian exponential diffusion kernel K = e^(beta L) over differential expression (Nitsch 2009, average rank 8/100 vs 17/100 for direct-neighbor-only).
- Exploit synthetic lethality for drug targets: find gene S synthetic-lethal with an undruggable cancer gene C (BRCA1/2-PARP1, KRAS RNAi screens) so a drug on druggable S kills only C-mutant cells; note only about 3000 of roughly 25000 human proteins have a druggable hydrophobic pocket and under 10% are extracellular.
- Pick the right null to test significance: configuration model (stub-matching, Molloy-Reed) or degree-preserving edge shuffle (Milo 2002; 15000 shuffles in the Mukhtar 2011 pathogen-target test) for abstract graphs, but MpK/stickiness biophysics models for physical PPI networks.

## Watch out

- The book is yeast-and-fly-centric with 2013-era data; interactome FP rates (45-90%), STRING scores, and OMIM counts (about 21000 entries, 2600 disease genes) are dated, and any topology claim built on a single high-throughput screen is likely an artifact, not biology.
- Power-law / scale-free fits are contested; the authors prefer the term power-law and note fits span only 1-2 decades of degree, and Wagner's preferential-attachment confirmation used error-prone data that Middendorf later rejected for DMC. Do not over-read a straight line on a log-log plot.
- FBA yields a flux polytope, not a unique flux: the LP optimum is objective-dependent (you choose biomass), steady state assumes no regulation, and MOMA/monochromaticity results are model artifacts as much as measurements.
- Disease-gene prediction, comorbidity, and synthetic-lethal calls are hypothesis generators validated only in-silico or in small cohorts (the breast-cancer 256-hub signature reaches only about 71% 10-year survival prediction); they are not clinical tools.
- This is math and biology, not runnable code: no software beyond named references (COBRA, STRING, MCL), no pipelines, and the graph theory is applied to molecules, so porting biophysical stickiness or gene-duplication models to computer or social networks needs care, they have no direct analog.
