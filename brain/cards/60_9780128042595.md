# 60. Emerging Trends in Applications and Infrastructures for Computational Biology, Bioinformatics, and Systems Biology

**ISBN** 9780128042595  |  **Lobe** bio-compute

> A 35-chapter edited volume from the BIOCOMP 2014 and 2015 tracks (WORLDCOMP, Las Vegas), collected by Quoc-Nam Tran and Hamid Arabnia (Morgan Kaufmann/Elsevier, 2016). Five sections span methodology and algorithms, bioinformatics and simulation, systems biology, large-scale data analytics, and clinical applications. Read it as a methods atlas: a routing surface of transferable computational moves, not a single-thesis monograph.

## Reach for this when

- Reconstructing structure or dynamics from sparse, noisy biological signal: residual dipolar couplings to a protein backbone (Ch6 REDCRAFT), methylation patterns to cell-division lineage (Ch1, MCMC plus ABC), coarse DNA markers plus capture coordinates to migration clusters (Ch11 ECON).
- Modeling a gene regulatory network as long-run behavior and needing to avoid the exponential transition matrix (Ch8 probabilistic Boolean networks, O(n^2) algebraic influence).
- Choosing a parallelism model for a spatial or lattice simulation and deciding when serial code forces a GPU rewrite (Ch5 OpenMP vs MPICH, Ch19 3D cellular automata).
- Measuring a genome or a stochastic grammar with information theory: Shannon entropy, KL and Jeffrey divergence, hapax/repeat indexes, recurrence distance distributions (Ch21 Infogenomics), grammar-space entropy vs accuracy (Ch9).
- Building and tuning a classifier on microarray or omics data with kernel and parameter discipline (Ch24 SVM embedded sigma tuning on TCGA lung cancer, Ch15 microarray feature selection, Ch20 SVM-Net complex detection).
- Formally verifying a safety-critical clinical workflow against written regulation (Ch31 YAWL plus LTL plus Divine model checking of FDA/AABB blood-bank mandates).
- Deciding how to discretize continuous variables before correlating, when the binning scheme changes which associations survive (Ch22 binning perils, StickWRLD).

## Principles

- Reduce the exponential to the tractable, then measure it. Ch8 dodges the 2^n by 2^n Markov matrix with an O(n^2) algebraic influence method (Boolean partial derivatives feeding a column-stochastic power iteration) and scales to 500-600 gene networks in minutes. The recurring move is a polynomial-cost surrogate for an exponential object.
- Anchor every measurement to a random null. Bernoullian/random genomes calibrate entropy and recurrence readings (Ch21), ABC and MCMC sample posteriors against generative models (Ch1), and a persistent random walk is the baseline motion for tissue CA (Ch19).
- Constraints are the data. REDCRAFT recovers a 2.084 angstrom backbone from only N-H and Ca-Ha couplings in two alignment media because dipolar couplings pin orientation against a shared Saupe tensor (Ch6). Minimal well-chosen constraints beat raw volume.
- Entropy ranks models but does not guard generalization. Lower grammar-space entropy correlates with higher RNA-model accuracy and with overfitting at once; the Typical Set Criterion has to arbitrate (Ch9).
- Pick parallelism from the memory topology, not the core count. OpenMP wins on shared memory past about 10 processes; MPICH wins distributed, and a ring beats a star for spreading message overhead (Ch5).
- Discretization is a modeling decision, not preprocessing. No single optimal binning exists; quartile and equal-count binning agree only near trivial residuals and diverge otherwise, so compute several and compare (Ch22).
- Formal verification makes a regulation executable. Translating FDA/AABB text into LTL over a YAWL state machine turns 'is this workflow safe' into a decidable model-check (Ch31).
- Fuse heterogeneous feature classes. SVM-Net combines 61 features (topological betweenness/degree/clique level, cellular localization, biological process, sequence) and beats single-signal complex detectors (Ch20).

## Moves

- Reconstruct a protein backbone from RDCs: run REDCRAFT two-stage, Ramachandran-filtered dihedral search then fragment elongation with search depth n, scored by RDC-fitness RMSD; feed {N-H, Ca-Ha} from two alignment media; validate bb-rmsd against REDCAT/PyMOL/VMD; tolerant to about 5 Hz noise (Ch6).
- Rank gene long-term influence: build the column-stochastic influence matrix from Boolean partial derivatives f(x with xj=0) XOR f(x with xj=1), power-iterate to equilibrium with PageRank teleport damping p=0.10; cost is O(n^2), not 2^n (Ch8).
- Parallelize a lattice CA: OpenMP '#pragma omp parallel for schedule(dynamic,chunk)' with chunk=10 for sub-100x100 grids; switch to MPICH MPI_Isend/MPI_Irecv nonblocking in a ring topology past about 10 processes, with Dijkstra-Scholten termination (Ch5).
- Integrate a coupled physiological ODE system: 21-compartment lumped electrical-analog CVSim plus Karaaslan renal model, Kirchhoff-current ODEs, 4th-order Runge-Kutta at dt=0.001 s, RAAS and ADH hormonal loops (Ch10, which also debunks the 8x8 water rule).
- Profile a genome informationally: compute H_L, KL and Jeffrey divergence vs a Bernoullian null, extract hapax and repeat words, indexes mrl/mhl/mfl and recurrence distance distributions; use suffix arrays for tractability; watch the 3-periodicity peak tied to coding density (Ch21).
- Detect protein complexes: LibSVM Gaussian RBF over 61 features, GainRatioAttributeEval (Weka) for selection, 10-fold CV, accept a predicted complex when Jaccard MatchKR >= alpha; 76/81 at alpha=0.30, beating ClusterONE, CMC, MCODE, PEWCC, ProRank (Ch20).
- Tune an SVM on omics: embed the sigma search in the pipeline (optimal sigma=32 for mutation, 64 for RNA-seq), use committee voting plus cross-validation, Bonferroni plus Welch t-test, topGO/Bioconductor GO enrichment; 94.9% vs 89.5% over linear on TCGA LUAD vs LUSC (Ch24).
- Verify a clinical workflow: model in YAWL, translate with YAWL2DVE (patched: = to ==, backslash to percent, string-to-integer map via random-int assignment), encode FDA/AABB rules as LTL, model-check in Divine over 9 states, 152 predicates, 56 constants, all inside OpenMRS (Ch31).
- Cluster spatial plus genetic data without a preset k: ECON expansion-contraction grid-density clustering (derived from CLIQUE) with gap-based partitioning to yield tightly-grid-dense clusters; classify pattern as trivial, local, scattered, continuous, or semicontinuous (Ch11).
- Stress-test correlations against binning: recompute associations under quartile and equal-count binning, visualize residuals in StickWRLD 3D; treat associations that survive only one scheme as artifacts (Ch22).

## Watch out

- This is an edited conference volume (BIOCOMP 2014/2015, WORLDCOMP), not a monograph. Chapter depth and rigor vary widely; some are short workshop reports (Ch25 mock circulation loop, Ch32 CT window moments). Route from it, do not cite it as a canonical reference.
- Every numeric constant is tuned to its dataset: sigma=32/64 (Ch24), damping p=0.10 (Ch8), chunk=10 (Ch5), 76/81 at alpha=0.30 (Ch20). These are case results, not universal values; re-tune before transfer.
- Tooling is 2014-2015 vintage: Maple 2015, Weka, LibSVM, MATLAB, OpenMRS, Divine, and YAWL2DVE (a 2010 translator hand-patched here). Verify current equivalents and reproduce before relying on any figure.
- Several 'first' and superiority claims (Ch31 'first verified blood-bank workflow', Ch20 beating five competitors) are author self-reports on small benchmarks; confirm against independent evaluation.
- Clinical and biological interpretation needs a domain expert. Ch10 debunking the 8x8 water rule and Ch28 synthetic Crohn's trial patients are modeling exercises, not clinical guidance.
