# 01. Critical Philosophy of Innovation and the Innovator

**ISBN** 9781786301475  |  **Lobe** philosophy

> A philosophical pry-bar for questioning a technology ontologically (by whom, why, for what) instead of bolting "responsible/ethical" on as a surface layer; reach for it when you need to argue that the converted operator, not the process or regulation, is the load-bearing intervention.

## Reach for this when

- You are framing an AI-safety or responsible-disclosure argument and need to show that process and regulation always lag the actor, so the real lever is the self-aware operator (Pavie: innovation always precedes the law).
- You want to interrogate whether a 'responsible AI' / 'AI for good' / fairness control actually reaches the system ontologically, or is just an ethics veneer on the same machine.
- You need generative, framing-breaking techniques for adversarial brainstorming (eidetic variation, epoche-driven astonishment, the bivium choice frame, multiplication of perceptions) to push past a null result or a default reward/objective.
- You are leading red-team or disclosure work and want a non-moralizing vocabulary for the operator's discipline: self-care as action and responsibility, what-depends-on-us, the master/mentor relationship, dialogue to be 'right together'.

## Principles

- Question the technology ontologically (by whom, why, for what) rather than adding an ethics layer on top of the same machine; responsible/social/inclusive innovation all fail because they never reach innovation ontologically.
- The actor is the lever, not the process: innovation always precedes the law, so no regulation, sanction, or 5-phase process (idea generation, feasibility, capability, launch, post-launch) can substitute for a thoughtful, converted operator. Maps directly to 's 'verification is the load-bearing stage' and the restraint ethic.
- Treat the sick system as a patient and philosophy as therapy: evil is not in things but in our judgments about them (Voelke, Epicurus, Hadot), so you heal by changing judgments, not by patching the object.
- 'Non' is suspension, not negation (Laruelle): anti-innovators only reinforce the power they condemn; the use and devotion, not the essence, is what needs review. Critique without becoming the mirror-image reactionary.
- Self-care (epimeleia heautou) outranks self-knowledge (gnothi seauton): caring for oneself is constituting oneself as a subject of action and responsibility, inseparable from caring for others - a benevolent individualism, not introspection or selfishness.
- The absence of resistance demonstrates the absence of thought: when an innovation devastates and imposes one way of life across every ecosystem with no friction, that frictionlessness is itself the diagnostic that thought is missing.

## Moves

- Run an eidetic variation on any artifact under assessment: vary its essence, manufacturing, materials, distribution, uses, abandonment, cessation, and recycling, and ask what it replaced and its full alternatives. Worked example - a phone exhausts rare earths, may emit harmful waves, hosts addictive games, is not recyclable: 'why should it be offered on the market?' Use this as a structured threat-surface and externality enumerator, not just an ethics exercise.
- Apply the epoche / bracketing move: suspend the 'natural attitude' (the reflexive 'is it profitable?' or, for an agent, its default reward/objective) to reach astonishment and access 'a new region of being' where new options appear. This is the philosophical twin of 'a null result means generate variants, not stop.'
- Use the bivium / Pythagorean-Y choice frame to force a binary decision into the open: the easy smooth road (profit, ship it) vs the long steep path (caring innovation, restraint). Name the fork explicitly before picking a side - challenge the framing.
- Deploy the three-stage historical schema (nameless existence -> profitable innovation -> crush-performance, with an unrealized fourth 'thought innovation') as a degeneration template to diagnose any tool, vendor, or discipline that has been hollowed out by incentive capture.
- Run a phenomenological workshop (after Spiegelberg): participants bring no materials, pool individual descriptions of the same object, and extract the invariants via 360-degree multiplication of perceptions. Maps to adversarial brainstorming and parallel-agent fan-out where each lane describes the same target.
- Separate what depends on us from what does not (Epictetus) as a scope-discipline filter on an engagement: act on the controllable, accept the rest, and tie it to the master/mentor relationship and the art of listening when leading red-team or disclosure work.

## Watch out

- Dense, repetitive, and light on empirical method: it gives you framing and vocabulary, not operational technique. Do not expect a procedure; pair it with the engineering-heavy titles for anything you actually execute.
- Treats 'capitalism' as a near-monolithic villain and leans on Silicon Valley as a blanket 'demonstration of irresponsibility.' Strip the polemic and keep the analytic frame; the monocausal villain is a confirmation-bias trap.
- The appropriations of Laruelle (non-philosophy), Husserl (phenomenology), and Hadot (spiritual exercises) are admittedly non-orthodox 'toolbox' uses, not faithful scholarship. Cite them as Pavie's instruments, not as authoritative readings of those philosophers.
- 'Conversion of the individual' as the decisive intervention can become an excuse to under-invest in process, controls, and verification. In  work the operator and the load-bearing verification stage are complementary, not substitutes; do not let 'convert the actor' override 'verify the finding.'
- The spiritual-exercise / self-care register can slide into the therapist voice the protocol explicitly forbids. Mine it for the action-and-responsibility definition of self-care, not for introspective moralizing in reports.
